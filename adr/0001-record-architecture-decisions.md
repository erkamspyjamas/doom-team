# ADR-0001: Record Architecture Decisions

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The Doom Team project needs to capture architectural decisions in a lightweight, reviewable format. This ADR establishes the convention and initial decision log.

## Decision

We will use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) stored in `adr/NNNN-title.md`. Each ADR will follow the lightweight template: Title, Status, Context, Decision, Consequences.

## Consequences

- ADRs provide a durable history of decisions.
- Decisions are reviewed alongside code.
- Maintenance overhead is low.
